05 / A few useful answers

Know what
the signal means.

What problem does QuietSignal solve?

Organizations can observe small pieces of the same supplier incident without knowing others are affected. QuietSignal explores how to correlate those observations and share an investigation brief without sharing full logs or unnecessary identifying details.

Does a shared pattern prove an attack?

No. Corroboration supports investigation. A legitimate update can also produce shared activity. Every evidence brief includes limitations rather than treating a threshold as proof of maliciousness.

Why use Chainlink CRE?

CRE offers a workflow model for coordinating external checks and confidential computation. Our CRE entry point compiles to WASM. Native simulation, deployed confidential execution, and protected participant delivery remain integration milestones.

Can I explore without an account?

Yes. This demonstration uses fictional organizations and observations. You do not need a wallet, API key, or deployment approval to explore the website. It does not collect or monitor your own network activity.

Is this already protecting real organizations?

No. QuietSignal is a research prototype. The local roster and source are synthetic, and the website does not provide production authentication, encrypted evidence delivery, or automated firewall protection.
